3. Meet the cardiologist for consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I visit a cardiologist?
You should consider visiting a cardiologist if you experience symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath or irregular heartbeat.
What tests may be required?
Depending on your symptoms, your doctor may recommend ECG, echocardiography or other cardiac evaluations.
Can I book an appointment online?
Yes. Click the Book Appointment button to schedule your visit.
