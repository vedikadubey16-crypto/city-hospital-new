Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit a dentist?
It is generally recommended to have a dental check-up every six months. Regular visits can help detect problems at an early stage.
Is teeth cleaning painful?
Professional teeth cleaning is generally comfortable. Some patients may experience mild sensitivity during or after cleaning.
When is a root canal needed?
A root canal may be required when the inside of a tooth becomes infected, inflamed or severely damaged.
