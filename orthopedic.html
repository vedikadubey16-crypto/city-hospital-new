About Orthopedics

Our Orthopedics Department provides comprehensive care for conditions affecting bones, joints, muscles, ligaments and other parts of the musculoskeletal system. Our experienced orthopedic specialists focus on accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

We provide personalized orthopedic care to help patients manage pain, recover from injuries and improve mobility while maintaining high standards of patient safety and comfort.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I visit an orthopedic doctor?
You should consider visiting an orthopedic doctor for persistent bone or joint pain, difficulty moving, injuries, fractures or other problems affecting your muscles and joints.
What conditions are treated by orthopedic doctors?
Orthopedic doctors treat conditions involving bones, joints, muscles, ligaments and other parts of the musculoskeletal system, including fractures, joint pain and sports injuries.
How are orthopedic problems diagnosed?
An orthopedic specialist may evaluate your symptoms and medical history and may recommend physical examination or imaging tests when necessary to understand the condition.
