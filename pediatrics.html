About Pediatrics

Our Pediatrics Department provides comprehensive healthcare services for infants, children and adolescents. Our experienced pediatricians focus on the physical, emotional and developmental health of every child.

We provide a safe, friendly and comfortable environment for children while ensuring proper diagnosis, treatment and preventive healthcare.

Vaccination

Child Illness Treatment

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my child have a health check-up?
Regular pediatric check-ups help monitor your child's growth, development and overall health. The frequency may vary depending on the child's age and healthcare needs.
When should children receive vaccinations?
Children should receive vaccinations according to the recommended vaccination schedule. Your pediatrician can guide you about the appropriate vaccines and timing.
When should I take my child to a pediatrician?
A pediatrician should be consulted when your child has persistent symptoms, unusual changes in health, developmental concerns or any condition that requires medical attention.
